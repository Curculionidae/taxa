How a record is placed

  • area country: an asserted distribution whose TaxonWorks area carries a country (level0_id; a subdivision carries its country's).
  • ISO code: an asserted distribution on a gazetteer, by the gazetteer's ISO 3166 code.
  • DwC country: the DwC country text matched to a country by name; the only rule for collection objects and field occurrences.
  • gazetteer name: a gazetteer named after its territory ("TAAF: Île Saint-Paul"), which can differ from its ISO code.
  • overseas GF, GP, MQ, RE, YT: an overseas department TaxonWorks files under France, by its areas, ISO code, name or coordinates; it has a checklist of its own.
  • ; part: for a country with selectable parts, the part the record counts for, or "no part".

Only Curculionoidea at species and subspecies rank, present records. Several TaxonWorks country areas of one country are merged into one checklist.
